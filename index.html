About

Background

I am a PhD researcher at the Human-Centered Technologies for Learning (HCTL) group at the Technical University of Munich, supervised by Prof. Enkelejda Kasneci. My work sits at the intersection of educational technology, human-computer interaction, and machine learning — asking how intelligent systems can be designed to genuinely support learners rather than simply measure them.

Before joining TUM, I studied cognitive science and worked on projects exploring eye-tracking as a window into learning and attention. I am passionate about participatory design approaches that center the learner's experience.

Research

Interests

My research investigates adaptive learning technologies, cognitive load, and the role of physiological signals — particularly eye movements — in understanding and supporting learning processes. I am interested in building systems that respond to learners in real time, and in understanding the ethical implications of doing so.
